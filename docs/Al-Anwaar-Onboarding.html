Module 01 · Giving

One flow, under a minute

No cart and no checkout.
Giving is not shopping.

Step 1

Pick an appeal

From an appeal page, the zakat calculator, a campaign link or the donate button.

Step 2

Choose the amount

Suggested amounts, any other amount, or units such as food packs and Qurban shares.

Step 3

Choose how often

Once, weekly, fortnightly or monthly, or split a larger gift into instalments.

Step 4

Add the details

Name, email, and whether the gift is zakat, sadaqah, lillah, kaffarah or general.

Step 5

Pay

Card through Stripe, PayPal, or bank transfer with a reference shown on screen.

Step 6

Receipt and record

Receipt emailed in seconds, the appeal total moves, the gift saved to the donor.

Also built in: fee cover, anonymous giving, failed payment retries, donation widgets and campaign links

07

The money settles into Al Anwaar's own Stripe and PayPal accounts. We never hold a donor's money at any point. If a card is declined, the system retries, emails the donor a link to fix it, and flags it to your team — instead of the plan quietly dying.

Module 02 · Donor portal

Donors look after themselves

Every request below is one
your office stops handling

Giving history

Every gift they have given, by card, by transfer or in person at an event.

Regular gifts

Pause, change the amount, switch card or cancel, at any hour, without ringing you.

Receipts and tax

Any receipt re-sent in one press, and the whole year as one tax statement.

Their own details

Address, phone, email and what they want to hear about, kept current by them.

Fundraising pages

A page for a wedding, a walk or a birthday, counting towards your appeal.

Secure login

A one time code by email, or sign in with a Google or Facebook account.

Supporter pages come into a queue for your approval before anything goes public

08

The tax statement matters more than it looks. Most of those requests arrive in the same two weeks at the end of June, and today every one of them is a person in your office digging through orders.

Module 03 · Admin portal

Where your team runs it

Every action is logged
with the name and the time

Donor records

One record per donor, with every gift, what it was for, and every email sent.

Duplicates

The same person under two emails is flagged and merged, losing no history.

Appeals

Set the target, write the story, open and close it, and watch the total move.

Email campaigns

Sent to a group you choose: monthly donors, one country, or lapsed givers.

Enquiries

Events, volunteers, partners and messages, attached to the donor record.

Staff access

Each person gets only what their role needs. A volunteer sees no exports.

Dashboard: raised this month and year, regular giving, appeal performance, new and lapsed donors

Inside the admin portal

Bank transfers, matched for you

This is the part that replaces
the work you do by hand

01

Upload

Export the statement from your bank and upload the file.

02

Match

Each line is matched to a donor and an appeal by name and reference.

03

Check

Every match is shown on screen before anything is saved.

04

Confirm

You press confirm. Gifts are saved and receipts go out.

Nothing guessed quietly

Anything unsure waits in an unmatched queue until a person decides.

Cash and cheques too

Gifts given at events or in person are entered in the same few clicks.

One trail, plus Xero

One receipt history per donor, and an export in the shape Xero expects.

Income reports by appeal, by designation, by country and by date range, for your accountant

10

The preview step is deliberate. Nothing is written to a donor record until someone has seen it, because a wrong match reaching a donor as a receipt is much harder to undo than a slow queue.

Module 04 · Compliance

The ACNC records, in one place

Instead of spread across
separate spreadsheets

Registers

Responsible people, overseas partners, policies, risks and conflicts.

Statutory calendar

The AIS and every other date, each with an owner and an email reminder.

Document library

Policies, partner agreements and reports, versioned, with who read what.

Audit trail

Every action with the person and the time, exportable when asked.

Reports

Receipts issued, income by appeal and designation, activity by country.

To be clear

We are a technology partner, not auditors or lawyers.

The platform stores and reports on the records you already keep, and pulls the year's evidence together

11

Your auditor's compliance report is given to us and loaded in — we do not produce it. Nothing here is legal, tax or audit advice; what the platform does is keep the evidence in one place so the year end is not a scramble.
